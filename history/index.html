What we learned together

North Carolina

Quaker roots and the Piney Grove meeting house

Elders shared memories of the Radcliffe and Taylor families in North Carolina, their ties to the Quakers, and the Piney Grove meeting house that held so much of the family's early life.

A photo of the meeting house is on its way. If you have one, please send it.

A name with a story

Foster, or Foskey?

Family memory links the Foster name to Barbados. Some records spell it differently, and relatives talked about how names were often written down the way they sounded.

Still an open question. Records, letters or old documents could settle it.

Shelton family

From Faust to Shelton

Spencer shared that his grandfather was born John Faust. He took the Shelton name when his mother married Henry Shelton, and the family has carried it ever since.

Spencer is putting together a list of Cheryl's mother's brothers and sisters for the tree.

Baseball history

Ted "Double Duty" Radcliffe

Kendahl shared the story of Ted "Double Duty" Radcliffe, the Negro Leagues star who earned his nickname by catching one game and pitching the next. Plenty of us saw a family resemblance.

Is he kin? If anyone knows how he connects, tell us.

Passed down

The family remedies

Relatives traded memories of the herbal remedies the elders used, including one that healed a serious cut. Knowledge like this lives only as long as someone writes it down.
