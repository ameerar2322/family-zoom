Have an Ancestry.com tree?

If you or someone in your family has built a tree on Ancestry.com, FamilySearch, or MyHeritage, send it and we’ll add those branches here. Any one of these works:

  1. Export the tree file (best). On Ancestry, open your tree, go to Tree Settings, and choose Export tree. It downloads a file ending in .ged. Email that file.
  2. Share the tree. On your tree, tap Share and invite the email below as a guest.
  3. Send a picture. A screenshot, printout photo, or PDF of a family chart works too, even a handwritten one.

Send it to Cousin Ameer: ameer@simplicityitinc.com
